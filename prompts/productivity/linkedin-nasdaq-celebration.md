# LinkedIn NASDAQ Billboard Celebration Image Generator

## Overview
Generate compelling LinkedIn images that showcase someone celebrating in front of the NASDAQ billboard in Times Square, combining authentic photography with professional achievement messaging.

## Prompt Template

```
Create a dynamic, authentic-looking LinkedIn image that appears to be taken spontaneously in Times Square. The image should show:

**Main Composition:**
- A person in the middle ground, naturally pointing toward the NASDAQ billboard
- The iconic NASDAQ billboard prominently displayed in the background
- Company logo subtly integrated into the scene
- iPhone portrait mode aesthetic with natural depth of field

**Visual Style:**
- Authentic, candid photography feel - as if taken on the fly without preparation
- Dynamic and celebratory mood, not staged or overly corporate
- Natural lighting with Times Square's characteristic neon glow
- Slightly blurred background to emphasize the subject and billboard
- Portrait orientation optimized for LinkedIn

**Technical Specifications:**
- iPhone portrait mode quality and depth of field
- High resolution suitable for LinkedIn posting
- Natural color grading with Times Square's vibrant atmosphere
- Professional yet approachable tone

**Text Integration:**
- Display the following achievement text on the NASDAQ billboard: "[USER WILL SPECIFY ACHIEVEMENT TEXT]"
- Text should appear naturally integrated into the billboard display
- Maintain readability while preserving the authentic feel

**Elements to Include:**
1. NASDAQ billboard with custom achievement text
2. Person celebrating and pointing toward the billboard
3. Company logo (provided separately)
4. Times Square environment with natural crowds and lighting
5. Authentic, spontaneous photography aesthetic

**Avoid:**
- Overly staged or corporate-looking compositions
- Unnatural lighting or perfect positioning
- Cluttered or busy backgrounds that distract from the main message
- Overly processed or artificial-looking effects

The final image should feel like a genuine moment of celebration captured in the heart of the financial district, perfect for sharing professional achievements on LinkedIn.
```

## Usage Instructions

1. **Replace the placeholder text**: When using this prompt, replace `[USER WILL SPECIFY ACHIEVEMENT TEXT]` with the specific achievement being celebrated (e.g., "ABC Corp Goes Public!", "XYZ Stock Hits $100!", "IPO Success - $50M Raised!")

2. **Provide additional images**: Supply the person's photo and company logo as reference images for the AI to incorporate

3. **Customize for context**: Adjust the achievement text to match the specific milestone being celebrated

## Example Achievement Texts
- "ABC Corp IPO - $50M Raised!"
- "XYZ Stock Hits All-Time High!"
- "Company Goes Public on NASDAQ!"

## Output Expectations
The generated image should look like an authentic iPhone photo taken spontaneously in Times Square, with the person naturally celebrating their achievement while pointing toward the NASDAQ billboard displaying their custom text.
